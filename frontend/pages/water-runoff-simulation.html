Soil Management Recommendations

Evidence-based suggestions to reduce runoff and improve water retention

High Priority

Install Contour Swales

Create shallow trenches along contour lines to capture and slow down runoff water. This allows more time for water to infiltrate into the soil.

Runoff Reduction: 35-50%
Implementation Cost: Medium
Maintenance: Low
High Priority

Cover Cropping

Plant cover crops during off-seasons to protect soil surface and improve structure. Choose deep-rooted species that enhance infiltration.

Soil Protection: 90%
Implementation Cost: Low
Maintenance: Medium
Medium Priority

Mulching Practices

Apply organic mulch to reduce surface sealing and evaporation. Use crop residues, wood chips, or compost for best results.

Water Retention: +25%
Implementation Cost: Low
Maintenance: Low
Medium Priority

Keyline Design

Implement keyline cultivation patterns to maximize water harvesting and distribution across the landscape.

Water Distribution: +40%
Implementation Cost: High
Maintenance: Low
Low Priority

Agroforestry Integration

Incorporate trees and shrubs into farming systems to create windbreaks and improve microclimate conditions.

Long-term Benefit: High
Implementation Cost: High
Maintenance: Medium
Low Priority

Permeable Pavement

Use permeable surfaces in high-traffic areas to allow water infiltration and reduce concentrated flow.

Infiltration Rate: +60%
Implementation Cost: Medium
Maintenance: Low
